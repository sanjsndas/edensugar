    Common questions

    Can I republish your recipes?

    We’re honored you’d ask! Please email us for permission — we usually say yes with a link back. Full terms on our terms page.

    Do you offer baking classes or workshops?

    Not currently, but we’re planning small seasonal workshops. Drop us a note and we’ll add you to the interest list.

    I’m having trouble with a recipe. Can you help?

    Absolutely. Include the recipe name and what happened — we’ll troubleshoot together. Photos help!

    How do I subscribe to your newsletter?

    You can sign up on our homepage. We send one email a week, no spam.

    Privacy note: Your message goes directly to our inbox. We never share or sell your email. Read our full policy.
